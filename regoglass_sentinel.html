Analytical Thermodynamics & Radiative Transport Model

Detailed formulation of optical fiber solar delivery, regolith silicate melt kinetics, non-Newtonian viscosity variations, and real-time state observer inversion.

1. Radiative Pyrometry & Wien Displacement Coupling

The molten lunar regolith crucible behaves as a high-emissivity non-gray cavity ($\varepsilon_{\lambda} \approx 0.88 - 0.94$). As concentrated solar flux heats the ilmenite-rich basaltic melt, the spectral radiant exitance $M_\lambda$ peaks at a characteristic wavelength governed by Planck's law and Wien's displacement:

$$\lambda_{\text{peak}} \cdot T = b \implies \lambda_p(T) = \frac{2897.77\,\mu\text{m}\cdot\text{K}}{T_{\text{melt}}}$$

Integrating over the sensing bandwidth of the high-purity fused silica optical pyrometer arrays yields the total net thermal radiation captured through fiber bundles:

$$E_{\text{fiber}}(\lambda, T) = \int_{\lambda_1}^{\lambda_2} \tau_{\text{fiber}}(\lambda, t) \cdot \frac{2\pi h c^2}{\lambda^5 \left(e^{\frac{h c}{\lambda k_B T}} - 1\right)} \, d\lambda$$

Where $\tau_{\text{fiber}}(\lambda, t) = e^{-\alpha(\lambda) \cdot L}$ accounts for photo-darkening and thermal defect center attenuation over exposure time $t$.

2. Temperature-Dependent Silicate Density & VFT Viscosity

Lunar regolith (high in anorthite $\text{CaAl}_2\text{Si}_2\text{O}_8$ and pyroxene $\text{FeMgSi}_2\text{O}_6$) exhibits strong non-linear variations in density $\rho$ and dynamic viscosity $\eta$ across the $1250^\circ\text{C} - 1700^\circ\text{C}$ range. The liquid density expands linearly above liquidus:

$$\rho(T) = \rho_0 \left[ 1 - \beta_v (T - T_{\text{liquidus}}) \right] + \Delta\rho_{\text{phase}}(X_{\text{spinel}})$$

Meanwhile, dynamic viscosity follows the empirical Vogel-Fulcher-Tammann (VFT) model, controlling the ascent velocity of trapped volatile bubbles ($\text{SO}_2, \text{CO}, \text{O}_2$):

$$\log_{10} \eta(T) = A_v + \frac{B_v}{T - T_0}, \quad v_{\text{bubble}} = \frac{2 r_b^2 g_{\text{moon}} (\rho_{\text{melt}} - \rho_{\text{gas}})}{9 \eta(T)}$$

Under lunar gravity ($g_{\text{moon}} = 1.62\,\text{m/s}^2$), reduced buoyancy demands low viscosity ($\eta < 18\,\text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$) to avoid structural voids in structural habitat bricks.

3. Phase-Space Observer: $(\lambda, \rho)$ Inversion Engine

Traditional thermocouples melt or chemically degrade inside lunar slag. The RegoGlass Sentinel employs an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) observing emitted spectral bands $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ to simultaneously reconstruct true melt density $\hat{\rho}$ and crystallization fraction $\chi_c$:

$$\mathbf{x}_k = \begin{bmatrix} T \\ \rho \\ \chi_c \end{bmatrix}_k, \quad \mathbf{z}_k = \begin{bmatrix} \lambda_p \\ \Delta P_{\text{piezo}} \\ \alpha_{\text{fiber}} \end{bmatrix}_k + \mathbf{v}_k$$

When premature crystallite dendrites nucleate, liquid density sharply drops while radiant emissivity drifts, deviating from the nominal curve:

$$\Delta \mathcal{D}_{\text{state}} = \left( \mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{h}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_k) \right)^T \mathbf{S}_k^{-1} \left( \mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{h}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_k) \right) > \gamma_{\text{threshold}}$$

If Mahalanobis distance $\Delta \mathcal{D}$ crosses threshold $\gamma$, automated heliostat focal trim commands are computed within 25 ms.

4. High-Flux Fiber Delivery & Self-Healing Annealing

Raw raw sunlight ($1,361\,\text{W/m}^2$ in vacuum) is gathered by a 3.4-meter off-axis parabolic primary collector, focusing $12.3\,\text{kW}$ of thermal energy into bundled large-core ($800\,\mu\text{m}$) fluorinated silica light-guides:

$$\Phi_{\text{crucible}} = \eta_{\text{conc}} \cdot C_{\text{ratio}} \cdot I_{\text{solar}} \cdot \exp\left( - \int_0^L \alpha_{\text{rad}}(t) \, dl \right)$$

Solar UV solar radiation induces Non-Bridging Oxygen Hole Centers (NBOHC, $\equiv\text{Si}-\text{O}\bullet$). When Sentinel detects attenuation drift in the $630\,\text{nm}$ absorption band, it redirects a $450\,\text{nm}$ bleaching laser:

$$\frac{d N_{\text{NBOHC}}}{dt} = \sigma_{\text{UV}} \Phi_{\text{UV}} - \sigma_{\text{bleach}} \Phi_{\text{laser}} - k_0 e^{-\frac{E_a}{k_B T}} N_{\text{NBOHC}}$$
